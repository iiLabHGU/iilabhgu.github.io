IILab is always looking for motivated students who enjoy building real
systems — from sensors and embedded hardware to deep-learning models deployed
on production lines.

## Graduate students (M.S. / Ph.D.)

We welcome students interested in machine vision, industrial AI, LiDAR
perception, or smart-material systems. Projects are often carried out with
industry partners, so you will work on problems that matter in the field.

석사·박사과정 대학원생을 모집합니다. 머신비전, 산업 인공지능, 라이다 인지, 스마트재료 시스템 분야에
관심 있는 학생은 언제든지 연락 주세요.

## Undergraduate researchers

Undergraduate students at HGU can join through capstone design, independent
study, or as undergraduate research assistants. Many of our alumni started here
and went on to graduate school or industry — see the [alumni list](/people/#alumni).

학부연구생은 캡스톤디자인, 개별연구 또는 연구 인턴 형태로 참여할 수 있습니다.

## How to apply

Send an email to **[{{ site.lab.email }}](mailto:{{ site.lab.email }})** with a
short introduction, your CV or transcript, and the research topics you are
interested in.
